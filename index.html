The Cost of Clunky Web Tech

Why 68% of affluent clients abandon typical spa websites.

Your physical facilities are world-class. But when prospective guests encounter a slow, clunky website with confusing navigation and broken booking links, they leave and book elsewhere.

Generic Templates & Typical Agencies

Lost Revenue & Friction

  • Sluggish 6-8s Page Loads: Bulky WordPress themes with 30+ plugins causing high mobile bounce rates.
  • Broken Booking iFrames: Forcing guests into disconnected third-party software that destroys brand credibility.
  • Unreadable PDF Menus: Forcing clients to pinch and zoom on mobile instead of one-tap treatment selection.
  • Endless Maintenance Retainers: Constant security vulnerabilities, broken layouts, and plugin update crashes.
The Jade Cursor Engineering Standard

High-Converting Web Architecture

  • Sub-Second Pure Performance: Handcrafted HTML5 & CSS delivering 100/100 PageSpeed scores on all mobile networks.
  • Booking Engine Deep-Linking: One-tap service selection wired directly to Boulevard, Mindbody, Fresha, or Jane App.
  • Interactive Digital Menus: Accessible CSS accordions with transparent pricing, duration tags, and package selectors.
  • Zero-Maintenance Clean Code: 100% bespoke codebase with lifetime ownership, zero plugin bloat, and rock-solid uptime.
