The Friction & Lost Revenue

  • Sluggish 6-8s Page Loads: Heavy plugins, bulky scripts, and broken mobile viewports that frustrate high-net-worth visitors.
  • Disjointed Booking iFrames: Forcing clients into generic third-party software portals that shatter brand luxury.
  • Unreadable PDF Menus: Pinching and zooming on smartphones to read service lists instead of effortless one-tap reservations.
  • Vulnerable Plugin Bloat: Constant updates, security risks, and broken layouts that require endless developer retainers.
