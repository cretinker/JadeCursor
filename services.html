Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from spa & salon owners.

Will this work with my existing booking software (Boulevard, Mindbody, Fresha, etc.)?

Yes, 100%. You do not have to switch booking software. We integrate directly with Boulevard, Mindbody, Fresha, Jane App, Vagaro, Phorest, and any custom booking platform. Clients can click any treatment on your site and go straight to booking that specific service.

How much time will this require from me and my team?

Very little — roughly 2 to 3 hours total across the entire 4 weeks. After our initial kickoff call and collecting your photos/service menu, our team handles all the copywriting, design, coding, and booking system wiring. We do not burden you with technical tasks.

Do I own 100% of my website after launch?

Yes. You own everything — your custom design, text, photos, and website code. Unlike proprietary website builders that lock you into monthly template fees, you have complete ownership with zero ongoing platform lock-in.
