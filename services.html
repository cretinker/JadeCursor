Common Inquiries

Frequently asked questions regarding our services & process.

Why do you build exclusively in pure HTML & CSS without JavaScript?

JavaScript-heavy websites are notorious for slow mobile loading, battery drain, plugin conflicts, and breaking whenever browser updates occur. By leveraging advanced HTML5 semantics and modern CSS, your digital sanctuary loads instantaneously, requires zero ongoing plugin maintenance, and provides a peaceful, rock-solid experience for your guests.

Will our existing booking software (Boulevard, Mindbody, Vagaro, etc.) work?

Yes, absolutely. We seamlessly integrate with all industry-standard platforms. Rather than relying on clunky iframe embeds that look terrible on mobile, we architect intelligent deep-links and custom modal trigger endpoints that send the guest smoothly into the exact service or package they desire.

How much time will my spa management team need to invest?

Very little. We understand you are busy managing practitioners, guest hospitality, and daily clinic operations. Our onboarding process is designed to be completely calming: after one 30-minute discovery session and receiving your treatment list, we handle all copywriting, aesthetic design, code architecture, and deployment.

Do we own 100% of the website once completed?

Yes. Unlike proprietary website builder agencies that trap you in high recurring fees, you own 100% of your source code, design assets, and domains. We can host it on ultra-fast, modern cloud infrastructure (like Netlify or Cloudflare) for virtually zero monthly hosting cost.
