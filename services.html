Technical FAQs

Frequently asked questions regarding our code & delivery.

Why do you build in pure HTML & CSS with zero JavaScript overhead?

JavaScript-heavy websites are notorious for slow mobile loading, battery drain, plugin conflicts, and breaking whenever browser updates occur. By leveraging modern semantic HTML5 and advanced CSS, your website loads instantaneously (< 0.4s), requires zero ongoing plugin maintenance, and provides a rock-solid experience for your clients.

Will our existing booking software (Boulevard, Mindbody, Jane App, etc.) work?

Yes, seamlessly. We integrate with all major booking platforms. Rather than relying on clunky iframe embeds that break on mobile, we architect intelligent deep-links that route the user directly to the exact treatment or package in your checkout flow.

Do we own 100% of the website once completed?

Yes. Unlike proprietary website builders that lock you into recurring monthly fees, you own 100% of your source code, design assets, and domains with zero vendor lock-in.
