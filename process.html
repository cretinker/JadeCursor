Stress-Free Delivery

Our 4-Week Done-For-You Process.

Busy spa owners don't have time to manage complicated web projects. We handle everything from copywriting to booking integration with only 2 to 3 hours of your time needed.

Week 01 Days 1 - 7

Kickoff & Strategy Blueprint

We start with a 45-minute kickoff session to review your signature treatments, ideal guest profile, and existing booking software (Boulevard, Mindbody, Fresha, Jane App). We map out the exact structure to maximize your bookings.

What Happens in Week 1:
  • Strategic Review of Signature Treatments & Pricing
  • Booking Software Integration Plan
  • Custom Sitemap & Content Architecture
Week 02 Days 8 - 14

Custom Design & Copywriting

Our design team crafts every page to match your physical spa's colors and luxury atmosphere. Simultaneously, our copywriter writes all the text to present your treatments persuasively. You receive interactive design mockups to review.

What Happens in Week 2:
  • Full Desktop & Mobile Visual Designs
  • Complete Professional Copywriting (Done for You)
  • 1-on-1 Feedback & Revisions Review
Week 03 Days 15 - 21

Website Development & Booking Wiring

We build your custom website from the approved designs and connect direct 1-click booking buttons to each treatment in your scheduling software. We test every button on both iPhone and Android devices.

What Happens in Week 3:
  • Custom Website Build with Fast Mobile Loading
  • Direct Booking Deep-Linking for Every Treatment
  • Gift Card & Membership Sales Flow Setup
Week 04 Days 22 - 28

Final Polish, Training & Live Launch

We do a final walkthrough with you, set up Google Local SEO so nearby clients can find you, and launch your new website live with zero downtime. We then provide 30 to 90 days of dedicated post-launch support.

What Happens in Week 4:
  • Google Local SEO & Maps Verification
  • Seamless Live Launch to Your Domain
  • Ongoing Dedicated Support & Updates
