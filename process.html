Our 4-Week Sprint Methodology.

Luxury wellness leaders pay for certainty. We replace endless agency delays with a streamlined, transparent 4-week delivery system designed to eliminate client stress.

Week 01 Days 1 - 7

Digital Discovery & Funnel Friction Audit

We conduct a deep dive into your current booking analytics, customer demographics, and signature service margins. We audit your existing software setup (Boulevard, Mindbody, Fresha, Jane App) to identify where mobile visitors drop off before checkout.

Week 1 Deliverables:
  • Comprehensive Booking Funnel Friction Report
  • Target Client Persona & High-Ticket Service Focus Map
  • Technical Architecture & Integration Blueprint
Week 02 Days 8 - 14

High-Fidelity UI/UX & Design Prototyping

Our design directors translate your luxury brand positioning into a bespoke digital design system. We produce complete interactive prototypes of every desktop and mobile screen for your review.

Week 2 Deliverables:
  • Bespoke Color Tokens & Modern Typography System
  • Full-Page Desktop & Mobile Interactive Wireframes
  • High-Converting B2B Copywriting Suite
Week 03 Days 15 - 21

Pure Clean-Code Engineering & Booking API Wiring

We write every line of code by hand in semantic HTML5 and modern CSS with zero JavaScript overhead. We wire direct deep-links into your booking platform so guests can reserve treatments in one click.

Week 3 Deliverables:
  • 100% Handcrafted Zero-JS Semantic Codebase
  • Direct Booking Platform Deep-Links & Treatment Accordions
  • Sub-Second Asset Optimization & Edge Delivery Configuration
Week 04 Days 22 - 28

Performance Benchmarking & Production Launch

We run rigorous cross-device audits, verify 100/100 Google PageSpeed scores, configure local luxury Schema.org SEO, execute seamless DNS propagation, and conduct team onboarding.

Week 4 Deliverables:
  • 100/100 Core Web Vitals Audit Verification
  • Zero-Downtime DNS Deployment to Global Edge CDN
  • 30-Day Dedicated Post-Launch Concierge Monitoring
