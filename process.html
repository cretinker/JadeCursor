Predictable Engineering

Our 4-Week Sanctuary Sprint.

High-ticket wellness leaders pay for certainty. We replace endless agency delays with a streamlined, transparent 4-week delivery system designed to eliminate client stress.

Week 01 Days 1 - 7

Digital Discovery & Funnel Friction Audit

We conduct a deep dive into your current booking analytics, guest demographics, and signature treatment margins. We audit your existing booking software setup (Boulevard, Mindbody, Fresha, Jane App) to identify where visitors are dropping off before checkout.

Week 1 Deliverables:
  • Comprehensive Booking Funnel Friction Report
  • Target Guest Persona & High-Ticket Treatment Focus Map
  • Technical Architecture & Integration Blueprint
Week 02 Days 8 - 14

High-Fidelity UI/UX & Sensory Design Prototyping

Our design directors translate your physical interior, lighting, and botanical rituals into a bespoke digital design system. We produce complete interactive prototypes of every page for your review.

Week 2 Deliverables:
  • Bespoke Color Harmonies & Editorial Typography System
  • Full-Page Desktop & Mobile Interactive Wireframes
  • Editorial Copywriting Suite Tailored to Affluent Guests
Week 03 Days 15 - 21

Pure Clean-Code Engineering & Booking API Wiring

We write every line of code by hand in semantic HTML5 and advanced CSS with zero JavaScript overhead. We wire custom deep-links into your booking engine so guests can reserve treatments in one tap.

Week 3 Deliverables:
  • 100% Handcrafted Zero-JS Semantic Codebase
  • Direct Booking Engine Deep-Links & Treatment Accordions
  • Sub-Second Asset Optimization & Edge Delivery Configuration
Week 04 Days 22 - 28

Performance Benchmarking, Staff Concierge & Live Launch

We run rigorous cross-device audits, verify 100/100 Google PageSpeed scores, configure local luxury Schema.org SEO, execute seamless DNS propagation, and conduct private staff training.

Week 4 Deliverables:
  • 100/100 Core Web Vitals Audit Verification
  • Zero-Downtime DNS Deployment to High-Speed Global CDN
  • 30-Day White-Glove Post-Launch Concierge Monitoring

Who we work with and who we are not for.
