# Jade Cursor — Tech Web Design Agency Master Craft Playbook

## 1. Core Brand Identity: The Luxury Tech Atelier
Jade Cursor is **not a spa**. It is a **premier digital design & software engineering agency** specializing exclusively in the luxury wellness, spa, and medical esthetics vertical.
- **Visual Vibe**: High-end tech studio meets serene aesthetic precision (inspired by the refined engineering of Linear and Stripe, combined with organic luxury typography and rich forest-tinted contrasts).
- **Core Value Proposition**: We engineer high-performing, zero-bloat digital sanctuaries that eliminate booking friction and convert high-ticket wellness clients.
- **Target Audience**: Spa founders, resort directors, medi-spa doctors, and wellness enterprise leaders ($500k to $10M+ annual revenue).

## 2. Essential Tech Agency Architectural Pillars

### A. Proof & Tech Superiority Over Fluff
- **100/100 Google PageSpeed & Core Web Vitals**: We highlight sub-second load times and zero-JavaScript performance.
- **Booking Engine Integrations**: Deep-linking and API connections with Boulevard, Mindbody, Fresha, Jane App, Vagaro, and Phorest.
- **Interactive Device Mockups**: Displaying client spa websites in realistic MacBook Pro browser viewports and iPhone frames.
- **Outcome-Driven Case Studies**: Every case study is an agency results teardown highlighting *The Broken Booking Funnel*, *The Engineering Solution*, *The Tech Specs*, and *Measurable Business ROI* (e.g. +142% direct bookings, $480k corporate revenue).

### B. High-Converting Sales Assets & Pages
1. **Homepage (`index.html`)**: The flagship agency portal featuring clear positioning, problem/solution teardowns ("The Cost of Clunky Spa Sites"), tech stack grid, client proof, and featured case studies.
2. **Services & Sprints (`services.html`)**: Agency engineering disciplines, deliverables matrix, and 3 structured investment sprints (Refresh Sprint, Signature Atelier, Imperial Enterprise).
3. **Case Studies (`portfolio.html`)**: Deep-dive results teardowns for JVEE Wellness, Beauticians On The Go, Aruna Spa, Pee Skin & Med Spa, and Eden Spa.
4. **Proprietary Process (`process.html`)**: The 4-Week Sanctuary Sprint Blueprint and "Who This Is For vs. Who This Is NOT For" client qualification criteria.
5. **Free Spa Website Audit & ROI Calculator (`audit.html`)**: High-converting lead magnet with an interactive lost booking revenue calculator and 24-hour video audit request form.
6. **The Atelier / About (`about.html`)**: The "Jade" (wellness serenity) and "Cursor" (engineering precision) duality, clean code standards, and artisan leadership.
7. **Project Intake (`contact.html`)**: Strategic discovery questionnaire capturing modality, monthly visitor volume, budget, and timeline.

## 3. Strict Aesthetic & Copy Rules
- **No Em-Dashes**: Ban em-dashes across all headlines, pills, body copy, and CTAs.
- **No Treatment Menu Copy for the Agency**: We sell web engineering, sensory branding, and conversion optimization — not massages or facials.
- **Double-Bezel UI (Doppelrand)**: Machined outer shell with hairline borders, concentric radii, and soft ambient shadow lifts.
- **Button-in-Button Architecture**: Primary interactive buttons feature trailing circular icons that translate dynamically on hover.
- **Strictly Zero JavaScript**: All interactions (drawers, tabs, accordions, calculators, forms) are powered by pure semantic HTML5 and modern CSS.
