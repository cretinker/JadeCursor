Onboarding Questions

What to expect on your Discovery Session.

What happens during the 30-minute Discovery Call?

We conduct an open, non-salesy audit of your digital presence. We will walk through your current booking journey, pinpoint where visitors are dropping off, discuss your signature rituals, and confirm whether our studio is the right strategic fit for your goals.

Do I need to prepare photography and copy before we talk?

Not at all. If you have existing photography, we will curate and grade it. If you need brand direction or new imagery guidance, our creative directors will provide a complete photography moodboard and shot list as part of Step 2.

What if my spa has multiple locations or sister brands?

Our Imperial Digital Experience tier is custom-tailored for multi-location clinics and hospitality groups. We build multi-city branch selectors, unified booking portals, and shared brand systems with individual location schema.
